## 6. GitHub Sponsors — joining as an India-resident individual (not started)
1. **Eligibility:** you contribute to open source and live in a supported region. India is supported (GitHub changelog, 23 May 2022: bank account and tax residence in India).
2. **Enable 2FA** on your GitHub account (mandatory).
3. Go to `github.com/sponsors` → **Get sponsored**, enter contact info, and choose a payout method: **bank account via Stripe Connect** or a **fiscal host**. A fiscal host can only be chosen at sign-up.
4. **Stripe Connect:** your residence and bank account must be in the same supported region. Double-check your identity details, which are hard to change later.
5. **Tax form:** non-US individuals submit **W-8BEN** before the profile can be published. GitHub **does not withhold tax** and gives no tax forms to non-US developers. You handle Indian tax yourself.
6. **Profile:** a short bio, what sponsorships fund, up to 6 featured repositories.
7. **Tiers (optional):** up to 10 monthly + 10 one-time tiers, priced in USD. The price can't be edited after publishing.
8. **Request approval:** GitHub reviews it, which "may take a few days".
9. **Fees:** **no fees on sponsorships from personal accounts**; up to 6% on organisation sponsorships.
10. **For your CA:** payouts arrive as foreign inward remittances. Keep Stripe payout receipts (exportable as PDF) for tax and FCRA questions.
Sources: [setup](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/setting-up-github-sponsors-for-your-personal-account) · [tax info](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/receiving-sponsorships-through-github-sponsors/tax-information-for-github-sponsors) · [about/fees/regions](https://docs.github.com/en/sponsors/getting-started-with-github-sponsors/about-github-sponsors) · [India availability](https://github.blog/changelog/2022-05-23-github-sponsors-is-now-available-in-india)
