## Support NearBird

NearBird is free software (GPLv3). It is free to use and always will be, and there are no paid features.

If you find it useful and want to help with its costs, you can make a voluntary contribution:

You can also help by testing on your phone, reporting bugs, improving the documentation or sharing the source.
