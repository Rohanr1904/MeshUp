# 01 — Product Vision

## Product
Working name: Offline Mesh Messenger

## Vision
Build the easiest and most polished way for people nearby to communicate without depending on the Internet.

## Core promise
Create a communication network around you.

## Product thesis
Most messaging apps assume reliable Internet connectivity. This product treats nearby peer-to-peer communication as a first-class capability and uses the Internet only as an optional enhancement.

## Target users
- People at concerts, festivals and conferences
- Students and campus communities
- Travelers, trekkers and road-trip groups
- People in crowded areas with overloaded cellular networks
- Communities in remote or temporarily disconnected locations
- Privacy-conscious users
- Emergency/incident communication users

## Core experience
1. Open the app.
2. Choose a display name.
3. Discover nearby people and rooms.
4. Join or create a room.
5. Chat normally.
6. Messages are delivered directly or through nearby peers.
7. Messages are stored and retried when connectivity becomes available.

## Product differentiators
- Nearby Rooms
- Event Mode
- Mesh-aware delivery status
- Store-and-forward communication
- Privacy-first local identity
- QR contact verification
- Emergency Mesh
- Battery-aware networking
- Modern consumer-grade UX

## Non-goals
- Do not become a generic social network.
- Do not require a central account for core offline messaging.
- Do not invent custom cryptography.
- Do not expose technical networking details by default.
- Do not sacrifice privacy for growth analytics.

## Product principle
Simple on the surface, sophisticated underneath.
