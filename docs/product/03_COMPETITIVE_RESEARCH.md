# 03 — Competitive Research

## Reference products

### BitChat
Strengths:
- Mature offline mesh foundation
- BLE and Wi-Fi-aware transport concepts
- Privacy-focused architecture
- Store-and-forward and routing concepts

Opportunity:
- Modernize consumer UX
- Make rooms/events the primary mental model
- Simplify technical concepts

### Briar
Strengths:
- Strong offline/privacy orientation
- Bluetooth/Wi-Fi/Tor concepts
- Local-first communication

Opportunity:
- More approachable mainstream UX
- Better event/community experiences

### Bridgefy
Strengths:
- Simple Bluetooth mesh consumer proposition
- Strong "nearby communication without Internet" concept

Opportunity:
- Better privacy controls
- Better identity verification
- More transparent delivery semantics
- Open technical architecture

### Meshtastic
Strengths:
- Excellent long-range mesh concept
- Strong resilience and community

Limitation:
- Dedicated hardware is generally required

Opportunity:
- Smartphone-only experience for short/medium-range local communities

### WhatsApp / Signal / Telegram
Strengths:
- Excellent mainstream messaging UX
- Familiar conversations and interactions

Limitation:
- Core experience depends on Internet infrastructure

Opportunity:
- Bring familiar UX to offline peer-to-peer communication.

## Positioning

Do not position as "another encrypted messenger."

Position as:

"The easiest way to create a temporary communication network around you — without needing the Internet."

## Key product insight
The consumer does not care about BLE, routing, TTL or Noise.
The consumer cares that:
"Everyone around us can still chat when the Internet doesn't work."

## Competitive moat
The moat should come from the combination of:
- proven mesh networking
- excellent UX
- reliable store-and-forward
- privacy
- temporary room/event mechanics
- battery-aware operation
- strong physical-device reliability
